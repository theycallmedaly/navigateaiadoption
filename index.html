Before you head out...

Answers to the questions we hear most often.

Do you train AI models, or do you train people to use AI?

We train people. Navigate AI Adoption helps employees, teams, and leaders use existing AI tools more effectively in their work. We do not train AI models with organizational data or provide data-labeling services.

Do you train remotely?

Yes, we can lead trainings remotely for a wide variety of timezones.

If our employees already have access to AI tools, what does training add?

Access and adoption are different things. Providing ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, Claude, or other AI tools gives employees the capability to use AI, but it does not automatically show them where those tools fit into their work or how to use them effectively.

AI enablement helps employees recognize useful opportunities, practice with real examples, and turn occasional experimentation into repeatable ways of working.

Can't employees just learn AI on their own?

Some will, and those early adopters can become valuable resources inside an organization. But relying entirely on individual exploration often leads to uneven adoption. Some employees become highly capable while others remain unsure where to begin.

Training gives teams a shared foundation, exposes employees to capabilities they may not discover on their own, and accelerates the learning process across the organization.

Our team already uses AI. Would training still be useful?

Very likely. AI capabilities are changing quickly, and the way someone learned to use AI six or twelve months ago may not reflect what their tools can do today.

Training can help experienced users move beyond prompting and basic content creation into thought partnering, custom assistants, research, analysis, automation, and more sophisticated workflows.

How can AI training deliver a return on investment?

The gains do not have to be dramatic to add up. If 30 employees each recover just 15 minutes per week by using AI more effectively, that represents 390 hours of workforce capacity over a year.

Time savings are only part of the potential return. Employees can also discover better ways to research, analyze information, develop ideas, automate repetitive work, and solve problems using tools the organization may already be paying for.

How is this different from a typical "AI 101" presentation?

Our goal is not simply to explain what AI is. We want people to experience what it can do.

Sessions combine demonstrations, practical examples, hands-on experimentation, and discussion of real workplace applications. The goal is for participants to leave with a better understanding of where AI fits into their own work and the confidence to start using it.

How do you keep the training relevant to our organization?

Sessions can be tailored around the AI tools your organization uses, the experience level of your participants, and the kinds of work they actually perform. Depending on the program, we can incorporate relevant workflows, business challenges, examples, and use cases so participants can connect what they learn directly to their work.

For which AI systems do you offer trainings?

ChatGPT, Claude, Microsoft Copilot, and Google AI Tools (Gemini and NotebookLM)
