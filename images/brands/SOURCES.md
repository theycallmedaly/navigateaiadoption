# Brand asset sources

Original assets are retained in this folder. The site applies its monochrome treatment with CSS wherever possible.

- Meta: Wikimedia Commons copy of Meta's official company wordmark — `meta.svg`
- Snap Inc.: Snap's official Contentful asset — `snap.svg`
- Walt Disney Studios, Princess Cruises, Carnival Corporation, BlueTriton, and Mars Wrigley: Epic Reach's published partner-logo wall; `*-mark.png` copies remove only transparent source padding for optical sizing
- Epic Reach: Epic Reach's official site logo — `epic-reach.png`
- Hunt A Killer: Hunt A Killer's official storefront logo — `hunt-a-killer.png`
- Twizl: Twizl's official app icon — `twizl-source.png`; transparent monochrome derivative — `twizl-mark.png`
- K-Mars Optical: K-Mars Optical's official 2016 price-list PDF — `k-mars-optical.png`; transparent monochrome derivative — `k-mars-optical-mark.png`
- Yuga Labs: Yuga Labs' current site icon — `yuga-labs.png`; transparent monochrome derivative — `yuga-labs-mark.png`

Additional source copies are retained for future design options: `twizl.svg` and `yuga-labs-wordmark.jpg`.
